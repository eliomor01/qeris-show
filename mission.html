What we do

Autonomous drones, cooperating, unhindered by terrain and obstacles

The problem

The next generation of intelligent drones and munitions

Difficult to detect

Small, slow, and operating without emissions, including fiber-optic drones and gliders

Detection by RF and soft-kill jamming is ineffective; radar is limited

Terrain-hugging and hiding

Flying just above the ground allows threats to hide in terrain and background objects

Late detection and line-of-sight systems bring high collateral risk

Evasive maneuvers

Quick and unpredictable changes in speed, altitude, and direction complicate interception

Erratic movements reduce tracking stability and prediction accuracy

Coordinated swarm

Simultaneous attacks adopt different roles, including decoys, relays, strikes, and ingression tactics

C-UAS systems must handle simultaneous threats

Our goal

End-to-end c-uas kit

Full-chain solution: From detection to interception

Interceptors navigate within meters of the ground hunting for threats that hide behind terrain

Multiple drones maximize coverage and handle swarm attacks

  1. Detection & tracking: Detect incoming RF-silent sUAVs with low-cost sensor fusion across EO/IR and acoustic sensors, with coverage up to 1km
  2. Launch: Low-cost interceptors launched upon threat detection from ground launchpads
  3. Engagement orchestration: Central control continuously forecasts threats' likely paths, optimizes defenders' placement and engagement roles to contain inbound threats, and adapts approach to straight-line attacks or evasive trajectories
  4. Autonomous pursuit: Defenders autonomously navigate, track, and intercept assigned targets using AI
Low cost

Built on cheap, off-the-shelf airframes, sensors, and simple launchpads

Compact and easy to deploy

1 docking station to place on your asset - ground-emplaced or vehicle mounted. Small & lightweight
